## 6. Audio generation plan (Higgsfield `elevenlabs_v4`, max 2 jobs at a time)

1. **Candidates** (3 each, same test line): Val (energetic, fashionable, slightly overwhelmed; stab 0.4) and Sonny (smooth, confident, understated; stab 0.6, phone EQ for L8). Voice IDs are picked from `list_voices` at run time and recorded in the manifest. Saved to `audio/voices/ep03/candidates/`.
2. After your picks: L1–L9 + L6a as separate stems `audio/voices/ep03/L<n>_<speaker>.mp3`, with settings logged in the manifest `voices`.
3. Music: `elevenlabs_music_v2_5` instrumental, 34s, "cinematic Miami retro action-comedy, confident electronic groove". Generated as licensed output from the provider, not a sample of an existing track.
4. SFX: Ep02's library approach (synthesized pings/clicks/whoosh) plus native grok engine audio for S01/S13. Door, rustle, chime and card accent come from `mirelo_text_to_audio` if a native sound doesn't fit (about 0.2 cr each).
5. Mix to the Ep02 target: about -16 LUFS, peak ≤ -1 dBFS, dialogue intelligible over music.
6. All voices are stock synthetic voices, never presented as anyone's real recording.

## 7. QC gates (each clip, then the full cut; frames sampled at ≥5 timestamps per claim)

- **Character:** faces, hair (Noelle's curls compact, Val's copper bob), wardrobe, exactly 2 arms/hands, natural fingers, no stretching.
- **Sonny:** face never visible (incl. phone avatars), stays in the passenger seat, red suit + long black hair.
- **Ferrari:** fixed hardtop, wedge body, strakes, 5-spoke wheels, BBY TIGR legible in S13.
- **Environment:** "Val Cruz BOUTIQUE" only; grep the captions and overlays for "VIVA MODE" (must be 0); door/rack geometry stable.
- **Story:** dresses always supported; one card, gold key; drafts labeled Draft/Review, nothing shown as posted; Val frazzled until S06.
- **Marketing:** no follower/engagement/sales numbers animated, no guarantees, no Black Friday, no date/time; registration is the team-supplied link only.
- **Tech:** every render checked with ffprobe for duration (filtergraph label rule), 1080x1920, 24 fps.
- Only failed clips get regenerated; retired takes are listed in the manifest.
