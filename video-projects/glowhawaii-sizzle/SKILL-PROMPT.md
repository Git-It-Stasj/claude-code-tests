# Prompt: create the "orchid-overview-reel" skill

Use with `/anthropic-skills:skill-creator`. Paste everything below the line.

---

Create a skill called **orchid-overview-reel**. After an Orchid run, it turns the client's finished report plus their live website into a branded overview animation (16:9 and 9:16 MP4s) in The Architect's style. We already built this once by hand for glowhawaii.com, so the skill must reproduce that result for any client with the same quality bar. The finished example is in the repo at `video-projects/glowhawaii-sizzle/`. Read its `STORYBOARD.md`, `VERIFY.md`, `grid.json`, `testimonial.json`, and `scripts/` (`build.cjs`, `music.py`, `qa-text.cjs`, `qa-cuts.py`, `prep.sh`) first, then turn them into reusable skill assets rather than rewriting from scratch.

## Trigger and inputs
- Trigger: "make the overview video / reel for <site>", or after an Orchid run finishes.
- Report: `runs/<domain>/12-report.html` (folder is named after the website evaluated). Read it fully before storyboarding. The report embeds a `REPORT` JSON object; parse that instead of scraping markup.
- Live site: `https://<domain>`, captured fresh with Playwright every run.
- Output: `runs/<domain>/video/` containing `STORYBOARD.md`, `VERIFY.md`, `16x9/index.html`, `9x16/index.html`, `out/<domain>-overview-16x9.mp4`, `out/<domain>-overview-9x16.mp4`, and `qa/` (contact sheets and results).
- Optional inputs, asked for at the start of every run, never assumed: (a) a client testimonial (text) and whether it is approved for public use, (b) a client photo and its source. If there is no approved testimonial, skip that chapter and say so; never invent or reuse another client's.

## Fixed format (same as glowhawaii)
Build with the HyperFrames skills (`npx skills add heygen-com/hyperframes`, pin `hyperframes@0.8.222`), as editable HTML/GSAP with one paused timeline, local GSAP, and local `@font-face` fonts. Do not use HeyGen cloud or MCP tools. The pipeline is driven by one `grid.json` (BPM, beats, chapter starts and ends), and every beat position in the build and the music is derived from it.
- Brand: ink #070D1F, secondary #0D1530, gold #F0C040, electric blue #3D7FFF, light blue #6AA0FF, off-white #E8EEFF. Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (statements), DM Sans (body), JetBrains Mono (HUD), Josefin Sans 200 (wordmark, lowercase "the architect"). Tagline "Stay Vigilant. Stay Visionary." and subtitle "Orchid Orchestration".
- Motif: four concentric squares with a small X in the innermost square (geometry from the glowhawaii build). Electric blue to navy on the opening and chapter stamps; gold gradient (upper-left to lower-right, fading to dark) on the closing lockup. It opens the reel, stamps each chapter boundary, and resolves into the wordmark.
- Persistent HUD (crop marks, title, spec line with BPM, timecode, chapter progress ruler, chapter label). HUD flips ink/paper with the background.
- Chapters: 1 THE REAL SITE (real captures scrolling slowly, hero headline visible, desktop and mobile); 2 THE AUDIT (selection boxes drawn by a cursor, severity chips, an audit panel, then the diagnosis ladder); 3 THE FIX (paper-colored chapter: before/after wipes, then the Voice-of-the-Market quote cards under a fixed bold title "Audience Awareness"); 4 TESTIMONIAL (if provided); 5 LOCKUP.
- Tempo 96 BPM, constant groove. Original synthesized bed generated by `music.py` (no samples, nothing copyrighted); no risers, drops, half-time or density changes. Light UI blips only. Master to -14 LUFS and remux over the render.

## What to extract from the report (never invent)
- Findings: the top two High and top two Medium cards from the Conversion Killer Audit, with their exact titles, severities and "where" lines, in report order.
- Diagnosis: section title, the Level 1-5 names, which level the site speaks at and which the market sits at, and the sentences "Your market sits at Level X" and "Your homepage headline speaks at Level Y". Order on screen: Level of the site line first, then the market line below it. Do not hardcode Level 2 and 3; read them from the report.
- Fix: the current headline, the recommended headline (Rewritten Copy), the recommended CTA, and the expired-event Red Flag if present. If the report has no expired-event flag, replace that fix with the next High or Medium fix that has a clear before/after, and say which.
- Voice of the Market: all quotes with attributions exactly as written, ordered longest to shortest, with the shortest, most hopeful one last.
- Every on-screen string tagged `data-t="report|brief|hud"`; `qa-text.cjs` must verify every report-tagged string appears verbatim in the report.

## Live-site capture and annotation rules
- Playwright, desktop 1440x900 and mobile 390x844 at 2x; scroll to trigger lazy content; save full-page PNG tiles (no tile taller than about 8000 px), unedited.
- Locate each finding's element on the live DOM by text and role (hero h1, hero buttons, the event banner or card, testimonial cards) and record page coordinates for the selection boxes. If an element cannot be found, show the callout without a box and list it in VERIFY.md; do not guess coordinates.
- Compare the live site with the report: if the headline, buttons or flagged event differ from what the report describes, flag it in VERIFY.md and in the chat summary instead of silently substituting.
- Check mobile for fixed or sticky elements (the glowhawaii mobile site had a sticky "Book a Discovery Session" bar). Scroll past them at the hero-buttons finding so they do not contradict the finding, and report them.
- After-states (new headline, new button, removed block) are labeled annotation overlays ("AFTER · RECOMMENDED", "REMOVED"), never presented as the live site. Screenshots stay accurate and unedited apart from overlays. Use screenshots only for this audit context.

## Pacing rules (the client approved these; keep them)
- All text at least 24 px at 1080p; nothing overflowing or outside the frame; 9:16 keeps text inside y 168-1330.
- Diagnosis ladder: five levels fully settled at least 2 s, then the site-level line for about 2 s, then the market-level line for about 2 s.
- Before/after: each state at least 2 s, wipe about 0.6 s.
- Quote cards: 3 s each, hard cuts only between cards, slow zoom kept, title fixed in place.
- Testimonial: typed on with a block cursor, then held long enough to read the full text (budget roughly 20 s for about 110 words), client photo on the left in 16:9 and on top in 9:16.
- Hard cuts only in the quote cards; everywhere else transform one object into the next.

## Gates, QA and delivery
- Before building: show the storyboard (chapter beats, findings chosen, quotes chosen, anything not found on the live site) and ask me to confirm. Before any publish, confirm testimonial and photo approval.
- QA, in this order, with results written to VERIFY.md: `hyperframes check` (0 errors); `qa-text.cjs` over the whole timeline (0 verbatim mismatches, min font 24, no overflow); render; `qa-cuts.py` (cuts within 2 frames of grid; explain any scroll-jump detections); loudness check; one contact sheet per chapter in both ratios, which you must look at and fix any collision or cut-off before delivering; dwell-time table computed from `grid.json`.
- Deliver both MP4s with SendUserFile, a one-paragraph summary of what to change for a different cut (which file and which field), and all flagged live-site differences. Commit the video folder on a branch and push if the repo is git-tracked.

## Lessons from the glowhawaii build to bake in
- Author hidden states in CSS and give every visible-end tween explicit opacity; use `immediateRender:false` on repeated `fromTo`s of the same property; do not tween `letterSpacing`; animate motif wrapper divs, not `svgOrigin` on the outer svg.
- Never `pkill -f hyperframes` (it kills the shell). Render both ratios in parallel in the background and poll.
- Do not shrink or scale text during transitions (use fades), or it dips under 24 px.
- Keep the build deterministic and regenerate both `index.html` files from one `build.cjs` plus `grid.json` so a revision is an edit and a re-render, not a rewrite.
